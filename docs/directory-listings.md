# Directory & review-site listings — the corrected list

*Reviewed 2026-08-06, in response to the ~19-site list shared internally.*

**Verdict on that list:** it is aimed at the wrong buyer. Most entries are indie-hacker
**launch directories** whose audience is founders and developers discovering consumer/dev
tools. Knowella sells EHS and compliance software to safety managers at trucking,
manufacturing and construction companies. Those people have never visited launchingnext.com.

**Why listings matter anyway (the real reason):** listing and review pages are exactly the
structured, factual content AI engines cite, and ~40% of B2B product discovery now runs
through AI answers. When a safety manager asks ChatGPT *"best EHS software for trucking
companies,"* the answer is assembled from G2, Capterra and industry directories. **Not being
listed means not being in the answer.** Referral clicks are the smaller prize.

## The step that actually makes this pay

Being listed does almost nothing. Being listed **with reviews** converts: ~92% of B2B buyers
read reviews before purchasing, and review-site profiles with no reviews read as abandoned.

**Sequence:**
1. Claim/create profiles — tier 1 and tier 2 *(a few hours)*
2. **Ask 5–10 happy customers for reviews**, spread across G2 and Capterra — this is the
   step that makes step 1 worth anything
3. Batch the filler list if someone has a spare afternoon
4. Any listing that sends real traffic becomes a **Source** in the outreach app, so its
   contribution to pipeline is measured like every other channel

Steps 1 and 3 are copy-paste work. Step 2 is the one that requires actual effort, and it is
the one that matters.
